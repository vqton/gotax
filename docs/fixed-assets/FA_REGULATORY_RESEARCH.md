## 1. Thông tư 45/2013/TT-BTC — Hướng dẫn chế độ quản lý, sử dụng và trích khấu hao tài sản cố định

**Replaced by: Thông tư 147/2024/TT-BTC** (effective ~2025)

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Full name | Thông tư hướng dẫn chế độ quản lý, sử dụng và trích khấu hao tài sản cố định |
| Issuer | Ministry of Finance (Bộ Tài chính) |
| Number | 45/2013/TT-BTC |
| Date | 25 April 2013 |
| Replacement | 147/2024/TT-BTC (new regime for FA management) |

### Key Provisions

**FA Recognition Criteria (Article 2):**
- Tangible FA: physical means of labor, involved in many business cycles, retains original physical form
- Intangible FA: no physical form, represents value of investment (land use rights, patents, copyrights, licenses)
- Financial lease FA: assets leased from leasing companies, lessee has purchase option at end of term

**FA Categories (Article 3 — 6 types):**
1. Houses, structures
2. Machinery, equipment
3. Transport means, transmission equipment
4. Management tools
5. Perennial plants, working animals
6. Other tangible FA not in above groups

**FA that DO NOT require depreciation (must still track wear-and-tear):**
- FA fully depreciated but still in use
- FA lost before full depreciation
- FA not owned by enterprise (except finance leases)
- FA not tracked in accounting books
- Land use rights with long-term legal tenure
- Type 6 FA per Article 3

**Depreciation Methods (Appendix 2):**
1. **Straight-line** — annual depreciation = (original cost - estimated salvage) / useful life
2. **Declining balance** — accelerated; coefficient: 1.5 (≤4yr useful life) or 2.0 (>4yr)
3. **Output-based** — depreciation = (actual output / design capacity) × original cost

**Original Cost Determination:**
- Purchase: purchase price (ex-VAT) + direct costs (transport, installation, testing, professional fees)
- Self-constructed: actual construction cost + direct costs
- Exchange: fair value of asset received or given up
- Capital contribution: value assessed by valuation council
- Donation/gift: fair market value + direct costs

**Useful Life Table (Appendix 1) — key ranges:**

| Asset Group | Min (yr) | Max (yr) |
|------------|----------|----------|
| Buildings — solid | 25 | 50 |
| Buildings — temporary | 6 | 25 |
| Warehouses, bridges, roads | 5 | 20 |
| Dams, dikes, canals | 6 | 30 |
| Harbors, slipways | 10 | 40 |
| Power generators | 8 | 15 |
| Power/hydro/thermal generators | 7 | 20 |
| Machine tools | 7 | 15 |
| Mining equipment | 5 | 15 |
| Textile machinery | 10 | 15 |
| Garment machinery | 5 | 10 |
| Construction machinery | 8 | 15 |
| Road transport vehicles | 6 | 10 |
| Computers & management software | 3 | 8 |
| Management tools | 5 | 10 |
| Animals | 4 | 15 |
| Perennial orchards | 6 | 40 |

**Change of useful life:** can only be changed once per asset; extension cannot exceed technical useful life; cannot change profit to loss or vice versa.

### Transition: TT 147/2024/TT-BTC
New regulation replaces 45/2013 entirely. Key changes expected:
- Updated useful life tables
- New recognition thresholds
- Digital asset provisions
- Stronger linkage to Circular 99/2025 chart of accounts

## 2. VAS 03 — Chuẩn mực kế toán Tài sản cố định hữu hình

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Full name | Chuẩn mực kế toán Việt Nam số 03 — Tài sản cố định hữu hình |
| Issuer | Ministry of Finance |
| Document | Decision 149/2001/QĐ-BTC (31 Dec 2001) |
| Effective | 01 Jan 2002 |
| Status | Active (still current, referenced in TT 99/2025) |

### 4 Recognition Criteria (para 06)
All must be met simultaneously:
1. **Future economic benefits will surely be obtained**
2. **Historical cost determined reliably**
3. **Useful life > 1 year**
4. **Meets value threshold per current regulations**

### Classification (para 07)
- Houses, structures
- Machinery, equipment
- Transport means, transmission equipment
- Management tools, instruments
- Perennial plants, working/producing animals
- Other tangible FA

### Initial Measurement (paras 12-27)
- **Cost model:** historical cost (all costs to bring asset to working condition)
- **Purchase cost:** purchase price (ex discounts) + import duties + non-refundable taxes + direct costs
- **Self-constructed:** actual production cost; exclude internal profits, waste, abnormal costs
- **Exchange:** fair value unless similar asset exchange (then carrying amount)
- **Deferred payment:** cash price equivalent; difference is interest expense

### Subsequent Costs (para 23)
- **Capitalize** if: future economic benefits increased beyond originally assessed performance
- **Expense** if: routine repairs and maintenance

### Depreciation (paras 29-36)
- **Depreciable amount:** historical cost - estimated residual value
- **3 methods:** straight-line, declining balance, units of production
- **Useful life review:** annually at year-end; adjust if significant change
- **Method review:** periodically; change if pattern of benefits changes
- **Start:** when asset is available for use
- Component approach: major components with different useful lives depreciated separately

### Derecognition (paras 37-38)
- On disposal or when no future economic benefits expected
- Gain/loss = net disposal proceeds - carrying amount; recognized in P&L

### Disclosure (paras 39-40)
Per class of tangible FA:
- Measurement basis for gross carrying amount
- Depreciation method, useful lives/rates
- Gross carrying amount, accumulated depreciation, impairment at beginning/end of period
- Reconciliation: additions, disposals, acquisitions through business combinations, revaluation increases/decreases, impairment losses, depreciation, other changes

## 3. VAS 04 — Chuẩn mực kế toán Tài sản cố định vô hình

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Full name | Chuẩn mực kế toán Việt Nam số 04 — Tài sản cố định vô hình |
| Issuer | Ministry of Finance |
| Document | Decision 149/2001/QĐ-BTC (31 Dec 2001) |
| Effective | 01 Jan 2002 |
| Status | Active |

### Definition (para 06)
Intangible FA: identifiable non-monetary asset without physical substance, held for production, supply of goods/services, rental, or administrative purposes.

### Identifiability (paras 09-10)
Can be separately identified (sold, rented, exchanged) vs goodwill

### Control (paras 11-14)
Enterprise has power to obtain future economic benefits and restrict others' access. Usually from legal rights (patents, copyrights, licenses).

### 4 Recognition Criteria (para 16)
Same as VAS 03:
1. Future economic benefits
2. Cost reliably measured
3. Useful life > 1 year
4. Meets value threshold

### Recognition examples (para 07)
Land use rights (finite term), computer software, patents, copyrights, fishing licenses, import/export quotas, franchise licenses, customer relationships (only if legally protected)

### Excluded from recognition
- Internally generated goodwill (para 33)
- Training costs, advertising, business start-up costs (para 48)
- Internally generated brands, mastheads, customer lists (para 42)
- Research phase costs (para 38) — ALWAYS expense

### Development Phase (para 40)
Capitalize if ALL 7 conditions met:
- Technical feasibility
- Intention to complete
- Ability to use/sell
- Generate future economic benefits
- Resources available to complete
- Costs reliably measurable
- Meets useful life & value criteria

### Useful Life & Amortization

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| Maximum useful life | 20 years (para 54) |
| Exceptions | Can exceed 20 years with reliable evidence, must disclose reasons |
| Amortization start | When available for use |
| 3 methods | Straight-line, declining balance, units of production |
| Residual value | Zero unless third party committed to purchase or active market exists |
| Review | Annually — useful life AND amortization method (para 65) |

### Subsequent Costs (para 50)
Capitalize only if BOTH:
1. Asset will generate additional future economic benefits
2. Cost reliably measured and attributable to specific intangible

### Derecognition (paras 68-69)
- On disposal or when no future economic benefits expected
- Gain/loss = proceeds - carrying amount - costs; recognized in P&L

### Disclosure (para 70)
Per class of intangible (separating internally generated vs acquired):
- Useful life/amortization rate
- Amortization method
- Gross carrying amount, accumulated amortization, impairment at beginning/end
- Line items in P&L for amortization
- Reconciliation of carrying amount
- Impairment details
- Description, carrying amount, remaining amortization period of material intangibles
- Intangibles pledged as security
- Commitments for future acquisition

## 4. Circular 99/2025/TT-BTC — Chart of Accounts for Fixed Assets

| Issuer | Ministry of Finance |
| Number | 99/2025/TT-BTC |
| Effective | 01 Jan 2026 |
| Replaces | Thông tư 200/2014/TT-BTC |
| Applies to | All Vietnamese enterprises |

### FA-Related Accounts

| Account | Name | Description |
|---------|------|-------------|
| **211** | TSCĐ hữu hình | Tangible FA — historical cost |
| **212** | TSCĐ thuê tài chính | Finance lease FA |

| **214** | Hao mòn TSCĐ | Accumulated depreciation (2141/2/3 for tangible/finance/intangible) |
| **215** | Tài sản sinh học | Biological assets (NEW account) |
| **241** | XDCB dở dang | Construction in progress (2411 - land acquisition; 2412 - construction; 2413 - periodic repairs; 2414 - upgrades) |
| **242** | Chi phí chờ phân bổ | Prepaid expenses / deferred costs |
| **229** | Dự phòng tổn thất tài sản | Asset impairment provisions |

### Related Expense Accounts

| Account | Name | FA Usage |
|---------|------|----------|
| **623** | Chi phí sử dụng máy thi công | Equipment use costs (construction) |
| **627** | Chi phí sản xuất chung | Production overhead — FA depreciation |
| **641** | Chi phí bán hàng | Selling expenses — FA depreciation |
| **642** | Chi phí quản lý doanh nghiệp | Admin expenses — FA depreciation |
| **811** | Chi phí khác | Other expenses — FA disposal loss, impairment |
| **711** | Thu nhập khác | Other income — FA disposal gain |

### Key Accounting Rules (TT 99 FA section):

**Original cost changes only permitted for:**
- Upgrades/improvements extending useful life or increasing capacity
- Component replacement extending useful life
- Improvements significantly increasing product quality
- New production processes reducing operating costs
- Dismantling components
- State-mandated revaluation

**Maintenance vs Capitalization:**
- Routine repairs/maintenance → expense immediately (TK 623/627/641/642)
- Periodic major maintenance → capitalize via TK 242, amortize until next maintenance
- Upgrades/improvements → add to TK 211 original cost

**Depreciation posting:**
- Production FA → TK 627
- Selling FA → TK 641  
- Admin FA → TK 642
- Construction FA → TK 241 (capitalized)
- Leased FA → same as owned (TK 212)

**Disposal/Liquidation:**
- Remove original cost (Có TK 211/212/213)
- Remove accumulated depreciation (Nợ TK 214)
- Residual value → TK 811 (loss) or TK 711 (gain)
- Proceeds → TK 711 (income)

**New: TK 215 — Biological Assets**
- Perennial plants for periodic harvest
- Working animals
- Follows similar depreciation rules as tangible FA

## 5. Decree 123/2020/ND-CP — E-invoice Requirements for FA

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Full name | Nghị định quy định về hóa đơn, chứng từ |
| Issuer | Government |
| Number | 123/2020/NĐ-CP |
| Date | 19 Oct 2020 |
| Effective | 01 Jul 2022 (full) |
| Status | Active (replaced earlier e-invoice decrees) |

### Key FA-Related Provisions

**When e-invoice IS required for FA transactions:**
- Sale of FA between independent entities (like normal goods) — Article 4
- Transfer between independent accounting units within same organization — Article 9.2(h)
- FA contributed as capital to joint venture by individuals/organizations — Article 9.2(e) exceptions apply

**When e-invoice is NOT required:**
- Capital contribution of assets to establish an enterprise (use minutes of capital contribution, asset delivery records, valuation minutes, and origin documents) — Article 9.2(e)
- Asset transfer between dependent cost-accounting member units within same organization (use internal transfer order + origin documents) — Article 9.2(g)

**E-invoice format requirements:**
- XML format per tax authority standards (Article 12)
- Must include: seller/buyer info, item description, quantity, unit price, total amount, tax rate, digital signature
- For FA transactions: detailed description sufficient for FA record creation

**Invoice timing for FA sales (Article 9.1):**
- Time of invoice = time of ownership/use rights transfer to buyer
- Regardless of payment receipt

**Authentication:**
- Enterprises can issue e-invoices with or without tax authority's code
- Must have digital signature
- Must transmit data to tax authorities

| Number | 200/2014/TT-BTC |

| Replaced by | 99/2025/TT-BTC (from 1 Jan 2026) |
| Applies to | All enterprises (large + SMEs with IFRS adoption) |

### TT 133/2016 (SME Regime — simplified, still active alongside TT 99)

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Full name | Chế độ kế toán doanh nghiệp nhỏ và vừa |
| Number | 133/2016/TT-BTC |
| Effective | 01 Jan 2017 |
| Applies to | Small and medium enterprises (by law 2014 criteria) |

### FA Differences: TT 200 vs TT 133

| Feature | TT 200 (now TT 99) | TT 133 |
|---------|-------------------|--------|
| FA accounts | 211, 212, 213, 214 (sub-accounts by type) | Same core accounts, simplified sub-accounts |
| TK 241 | Detailed (2411, 2412, 2413, 2414) | Simplified (2411, 2412) |
| TK 242 | Yes — prepaid expenses | Yes — simplified |
| TK 2141/2/3 | Separate for tangible/finance/intangible | Combined |
| Depreciation methods | Straight-line, declining, output | Same 3 methods |
| FA minimum value | 30 million VND | Same |
| FA disposal | TK 811/711 | Same |

### FA Provisions Common to Both Regimes

**Minimum Recognition Threshold (historical, confirmed in practice):**
- Value: ≥ 30,000,000 VND
- Useful life: > 1 year
- Must meet VAS 03/04 criteria

**Depreciation:**
- Straight-line is default
- Declining balance allowed with conditions (high-tech, innovation)
- Output-based for natural resource assets
- Full-year depreciation in year of acquisition (some interpretations: from month of use)

**FA Records Required:**
- FA increase record (Biên bản giao nhận TSCĐ)
- FA liquidation record (Biên bản thanh lý TSCĐ)
- FA transfer record
- FA inventory record
- FA tracking card (Thẻ TSCĐ) — per individual asset

**Period-End Procedures:**
- Annual FA physical inventory
- FA useful life review
- Impairment assessment (not mandatory under VAS, but under Circular 228/2009 for listed companies)
- Depreciation recalculation if useful life changes

## FA Lifecycle Summary — All Regulations

### 1. Acquisition
- Determine original cost per TT 45 (or 147) Art. 4-5, VAS 03 para 12-27
- Create FA record (Biên bản giao nhận TSCĐ)
- Post: Nợ TK 211/213, Có TK 111/112/331/341
- Allocate to correct FA category per COA

### 2. Depreciation
- Choose method per TT 45 Appendix 2 (or TT 147)
- Set useful life within Appendix 1 range
- Start depreciation when FA available for use
- Monthly: Nợ TK 627/641/642, Có TK 214

### 3. Maintenance & Upgrades
- Routine repairs → expense immediately (TK 627/641/642)
- Periodic major maintenance → TK 242, amortize over period
- Upgrades extending life/increasing capacity → capitalize (increase TK 211)

### 4. Revaluation
- Only when state-mandated (VAS 03 para 28)
- Adjust TK 211, TK 214, and record difference

### 5. Impairment
- Assess at each reporting date
- If recoverable amount < carrying amount → recognize loss
- Per VAS rules on asset impairment (Circular 228/2009 for listed cos)

### 6. Transfer
- Within same legal entity (dependent units): internal order, no e-invoice
- Between independent entities: e-invoice required
- Post: Nợ TK 214, Nợ TK 136/411, Có TK 211

### 7. Disposal/Liquidation
- Create liquidation committee
- Record: Biên bản thanh lý TSCĐ
- Remove asset and accumulated depreciation
- Recognize gain/loss in TK 711/811
- Issue e-invoice for sale proceeds

### 8. Reporting
- FA schedule (Bảng tăng giảm TSCĐ) — monthly/quarterly/annually
- FA tracking card (Thẻ TSCĐ) — per asset
- Notes to financial statements (Thuyết minh BCTC) — section on FA
- FA disclosure per VAS 03 para 39, VAS 04 para 70
